Lessons Built Into This App

  • Today matters more than seeing the whole month.
  • Fixed items and flex items need different treatment.
  • The lift guide should be inside the workout flow, not a separate memory burden.
  • Colorblind-safe labels must carry meaning by text and shape, not color.
  • The app should preserve momentum with FULL / MIN / RECOVERY rather than pass/fail tracking.
